## fl-family-law

- **2023 alimony reform numbers** (`property-and-alimony.*`; q006, q007 and e01 depend on them; q007 entirely):
  - marriage-length bands of under 10, 10–20 and 20+ years;
  - a 5-year cap on rehabilitative alimony;
  - no durational alimony for marriages under 3 years;
  - durational length capped at 50/60/75% of the marriage;
  - durational amount capped at the lesser of need or 35% of the difference in net incomes.
- **Older awards** (q008): whether the 2023 reform leaves permanent alimony awarded before July 1, 2023 in place.
- **Equal time-sharing presumption:** whether it applies to modifications (q009). The wording of the required written findings is paraphrased.
- **Parenting-plan modification:** "unanticipated" was removed from the change-of-circumstances test in 2023.
- **Cases:** Kaaa v. Kaaa and § 61.075(6)(a)1; Pimm v. Pimm; Padgett (least restrictive means).
- **Child support:**
  - 24 months of retroactive support (§ 61.30(17));
  - the 15%-or-$50 modification threshold.
- **Premarital agreements:** § 61.079 applies to agreements signed on or after 10/1/2007; Florida's extra enforcement defenses.
- **Domestic violence:** the 15-day ex parte injunction and firearm surrender under § 790.233. The crime level for violating an injunction is left out.
- **Dependency:** the 21-day petition deadline after the shelter hearing.
- **Residency:** the § 61.052(2) subsection on corroboration.
- **Statute numbers:** UCCJEA sections (§§ 61.514–.521) are from memory. UIFSA is cited only as ch. 88.
- **Added topic:** `fl-family-law.interstate-jurisdiction`, now in the lecture taxonomy.
