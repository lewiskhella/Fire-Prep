## fl-family-law

- **2023 alimony reform numbers** (`property-and-alimony.*`; q006, q007 and e01 depend on them; q007 entirely):
  - marriage-length bands of under 10, 10–20 and 20+ years;
  - a 5-year cap on rehabilitative alimony;
  - no durational alimony for marriages under 3 years;
  - durational length capped at 50/60/75% of the marriage;
  - durational amount capped at the lesser of need or 35% of the difference in net incomes.
- **Older awards** (q008): whether the 2023 reform leaves permanent alimony awarded before July 1, 2023 in place.
- **Equal time-sharing presumption:** whether it applies to modifications (q009). The wording of the required written findings is paraphrased.
- **Parenting-plan modification:** "unanticipated" was removed from the change-of-circumstances test in 2023.
- **Cases:** Kaaa v. Kaaa and § 61.075(6)(a)1; Pimm v. Pimm; Padgett (least restrictive means).
- **Child support:**
  - 24 months of retroactive support (§ 61.30(17));
  - the 15%-or-$50 modification threshold.
- **Premarital agreements:** § 61.079 applies to agreements signed on or after 10/1/2007; Florida's extra enforcement defenses.
- **Domestic violence:** the 15-day ex parte injunction and firearm surrender under § 790.233. The crime level for violating an injunction is left out.
- **Dependency:** the 21-day petition deadline after the shelter hearing.
- **Residency:** the § 61.052(2) subsection on corroboration.
- **Statute numbers:** UCCJEA sections (§§ 61.514–.521) are from memory. UIFSA is cited only as ch. 88.
- **Added topic:** `fl-family-law.interstate-jurisdiction`, now in the lecture taxonomy.

## fl-professional-responsibility

- **Rule 4-1.2(c):** limited-scope consent in writing.
- **Rule 4-1.5:**
  - written confirmation of nonrefundable fees (4-1.5(e));
  - the full 4-1.5(f)(4) contingency schedule and the 3-day cancellation right (confident);
  - the Art. I, § 26 waiver procedure (described generally);
  - the 75/25 fee-division split.
- **Rosenberg v. Levin:** quantum meruit after a without-cause discharge.
- **Screening** (moderate confidence): Florida does not let a screen alone cure a lateral hire's former-client conflict.
- **Conflicts:**
  - Rule 4-1.7(d), related lawyers;
  - Rule 4-1.8(j), insureds;
  - Rule 4-8.4(i), sexual conduct;
  - Rule 4-1.8(g), aggregate settlements (PR e01).
- **ABA 1.8(e)(3)** (gifts to indigent clients): whether Florida adopted it is not stated.
- **Candor** (moderate confidence): the "narrative form" wording.
- **Rule 4-3.5(d)(4):** post-verdict juror contact (q014).
- **Contact with represented parties:** the exception for notice required by law or contract.
- **Advertising:**
  - testimonials follow the 2013 rules; specialist claims are left out after Searcy;
  - filing exemptions;
  - direct-mail rules (q013).
- **Rule 4-3.4(g)–(h):** threats.
- **Rule 4-8.4:** the protected categories in (d), (g) failure to respond to the Bar, and (h) child-support nonpayment.
- **Duty to report:** the lawyers'-assistance-program exception.
- **Discipline:** rehabilitation required for suspensions over 90 days; 5 years before readmission after disbarment.
- **Unlicensed practice:** § 454.23.
- **Pro bono:** whether mandatory reporting still applies.
- **Trust records:** kept 6 years; how often reconciliation is required; Rule 5-1.1(b) and (c).
- **PR e02:** treats Rule 4-1.13 through the concurrent-conflicts rule.
- **Added topics:** `fl-professional-responsibility.trust-accounting` and `.discipline-and-practice`, now in the lecture taxonomy.
